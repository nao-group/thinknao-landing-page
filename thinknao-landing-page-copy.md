# ThinkNAO — Landing Page Copy

## Hero / Product Description

**Headline options:**
- "Master the CSCA. On Your Terms."
- "Your Path to a Chinese University, Simplified."
- "CSCA Prep, Built for How You Actually Study."

**Short description (tagline, ~1 sentence):**
The bilingual AI-powered exam prep platform built for Indonesian students conquering the CSCA — Maths, Physics, Chemistry, and Academic Chinese, all in one place.

**Long description (2–3 sentences for hero section):**
ThinkNAO is the AI-driven study companion designed specifically for Indonesian students preparing for the CSCA Chinese university entrance exam. Practice with a curated question bank matched to real CSCA question types, and when you've worked through it, AI generates new questions in the same style — so you never run out of practice. Every question, generated or curated, is verified for mathematical correctness before it reaches you. Study in English or Chinese, track your real progress, and walk into exam day prepared.

## Key Features

### 1. Unlimited, Verified-Correct Practice
Start with a curated bank of questions matched to real CSCA question types. Once you've worked through what's available, ThinkNAO's AI generates new questions in the same style — so you always have something new to practice. Every question, curated or AI-generated, passes an automated mathematical verification check before it ever reaches you. No guessing whether an answer key is wrong.

### 2. Full CSCA Subject Coverage
Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Liberal Art Chinese, and Science Chinese — practice questions built around the actual structure and difficulty of the CSCA exam.

### 3. Bilingual by Design
Study seamlessly in English or Chinese, built specifically for Indonesian students navigating a Chinese-language entrance exam — not adapted from a mainland Chinese product.

### 4. Adaptive Mastery Tracking
ThinkNAO learns where you're strong and where you're not, adjusting what you practice next based on your real performance — so your study time goes where it matters most.

### 5. Full-Length Mock Exams
Simulate real exam conditions with timed, structured mock tests that mirror the actual CSCA format.

### 6. Flashcards
Quick-review flashcards for formulas, vocabulary, and key concepts — built for fast recall, not just recognition.

### 7. AI Study Assistant
Get help exactly when you need it — grounded in verified answer keys, with guided hints instead of instant answers, so you build understanding instead of just copying solutions.

### 8. Community & Leaderboard
Compete with peers, track your ranking, and connect with other students preparing for the same universities in a dedicated Discord community.
